Born in a garage, built by riders

We started Spokeworks because good bike repair should be fair, fast and easy to understand.

Our story

Bilal began repairing friends' bikes in 2019. Word spread, the garage filled up, and the neighbourhood asked for a real workshop.

Today a team of four mechanics and instructors serves students, commuters and weekend riders, with the same promise: tell you the price first and explain the fix.
